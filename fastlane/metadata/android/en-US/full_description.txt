A customer-support console for people who ship apps. It pulls in the two places
users actually complain, Play Store reviews and GitHub issues, and groups them so
twenty reports of one bug read as one problem instead of twenty.

WHAT IT DOES
• Ingests Play Store reviews and GitHub issues for the apps and repositories you
  own.
• Clusters them into incidents, so a single bug is a single item in your queue.
• Lets you triage and reply from your phone.

TWO RULES IT IS BUILT AROUND
• **Nothing is sent to a user without you confirming it.** There is no auto-reply
  and no scheduled send. Every outgoing message is one you read and approved.
• **Clustering is deterministic.** Running it again on the same data produces the
  same incidents with the same identifiers. It never quietly reshuffles your queue
  underneath you.

HISTORY THAT ACTUALLY GOES BACK
The Play API only exposes about a week of reviews, so the app also reads the
monthly CSV exports from Play Console to backfill older history. Your incident view
is not limited to the last seven days.

YOUR CREDENTIALS, YOUR DEVICE
Your Play service account key and your GitHub token are stored encrypted on your
device and are used only to talk to Google and GitHub directly. There is no server
of ours in between, and no account to create.
